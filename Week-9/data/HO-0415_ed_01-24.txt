TITLE: Roof Surfacing Cosmetic Damage Exclusion Endorsement

THIS ENDORSEMENT CHANGES THE POLICY. PLEASE READ IT CAREFULLY.
This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the Homeowners 3 - Special Form.

CLAUSE 1. DEFINITIONS
For the purposes of this endorsement:
(a) "Cosmetic damage" means marring, pitting, denting, or other superficial alteration of the roof surfacing that does not prevent the roof from continuing to function as a barrier to entrance of the elements.
(b) "Roof surfacing" means the shingles, tiles, membrane, or metal panels exposed to the weather, including underlayment and flashing.

CLAUSE 2. SCOPE OF THIS ENDORSEMENT
This endorsement scopes exclusion codes E-21 and E-22 as applied to Coverage A - Dwelling. It applies only to loss caused by wind or hail occurring on or after the effective date shown above.

CLAUSE 3. EXCLUSIONS TABLE
The following exclusions apply to Coverage A - Dwelling:

| Code | Exclusion Description | Applies To | Exception |
| E-21 | Cosmetic damage to roof surfacing caused by hail | Coverage A - Dwelling | Does NOT apply if the hail impact penetrates the underlayment or causes water intrusion within 30 days of the storm event. |
| E-22 | Damage to metal roof surfacing consisting solely of denting, without loss of water-shedding function | Coverage A - Dwelling | None. Denting of metal roofs is excluded absolutely. |

CLAUSE 4. ROOF INSPECTION RIGHT
The insurer may inspect the roof surfacing within 60 days of a reported wind or hail claim. The insured shall not authorize permanent repairs before inspection except emergency measures to prevent further damage.

CLAUSE 5. OTHER TERMS
All other provisions of the policy remain unchanged.
