TITLE: Rental Dwelling Water Damage Limitation Endorsement

THIS ENDORSEMENT CHANGES THE POLICY. PLEASE READ IT CAREFULLY.
This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the Dwelling Property 3 - Special Form, issued for tenant-occupied rental dwellings.

CLAUSE 1. DEFINITIONS
For the purposes of this endorsement:
(a) "Tenant-occupied period" means any period during which the dwelling is leased to one or more tenants under a written or oral rental agreement.
(b) "Burst supply line" means the sudden rupture of any pipe, hose, or fitting carrying potable water under pressure within the described dwelling.

CLAUSE 2. SCOPE OF THIS ENDORSEMENT
This endorsement scopes exclusion codes E-61 and E-62 as applied to Coverage A - Dwelling for tenant-occupied rental dwellings on the dwelling-fire policy line.

CLAUSE 3. EXCLUSIONS TABLE
The following exclusions apply to Coverage A - Dwelling:

| Code | Exclusion Description | Applies To | Exception |
| E-61 | Water damage from a burst supply line occurring while the dwelling is unoccupied between tenancies for more than 30 consecutive days | Coverage A - Dwelling | Does NOT apply if the water supply was shut off at the main and the plumbing system drained during the unoccupied period. |
| E-62 | Water damage of any kind where the landlord failed to maintain heat of at least 55 degrees Fahrenheit during the months of November through March | Coverage A - Dwelling | None. Freeze-related losses without maintained heat are excluded absolutely. |

CLAUSE 4. LANDLORD DUTIES
The named insured must inspect the dwelling within 7 days of a tenancy ending and document the condition of visible plumbing. Failure to inspect voids the exception in exclusion E-61.

CLAUSE 5. OTHER TERMS
All other provisions of the policy remain unchanged.
