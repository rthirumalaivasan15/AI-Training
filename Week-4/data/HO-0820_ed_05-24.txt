TITLE: Water Backup and Sump Discharge Coverage Endorsement

THIS ENDORSEMENT CHANGES THE POLICY. PLEASE READ IT CAREFULLY.
This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the Homeowners 3 - Special Form.

CLAUSE 1. DEFINITIONS
For the purposes of this endorsement:
(a) "Water backup" means water or waterborne material which backs up through sewers or drains, or which overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.
(b) "Related equipment" means the sump pit, discharge piping, check valves, and battery backup unit serving the sump pump.

CLAUSE 2. COVERAGE GRANT
The insurer will pay up to 10,000 dollars per loss event for direct physical loss to property covered under Section I caused by water backup. This coverage overrides exclusion E-16 of endorsement HO-0304 while this endorsement remains attached to the policy. A deductible of 1,000 dollars applies separately to this coverage.

CLAUSE 3. EXCLUSIONS TABLE
The following exclusions apply to this endorsement:

| Code | Exclusion Description | Applies To | Exception |
| E-51 | Water backup caused by the insured's failure to keep the sump pump in working order | This endorsement | Does NOT apply if the failure was caused by a power outage lasting more than 12 continuous hours. |
| E-52 | Water backup occurring while the residence premises is vacant for more than 60 consecutive days | This endorsement | None. Vacancy beyond 60 days suspends this coverage. |

CLAUSE 4. MAINTENANCE DUTY
The insured must test the sump pump and related equipment at least once every 90 days and retain proof of battery backup replacement performed within the 24 months preceding a claimed loss.

CLAUSE 5. OTHER TERMS
All other provisions of the policy remain unchanged.
