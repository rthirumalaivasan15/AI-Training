TITLE: Ordinance or Law Increased Coverage Endorsement

THIS ENDORSEMENT CHANGES THE POLICY. PLEASE READ IT CAREFULLY.
This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the Homeowners 3 - Special Form.

CLAUSE 1. DEFINITIONS
For the purposes of this endorsement:
(a) "Ordinance or law" means any ordinance or law regulating the construction, demolition, remodeling, renovation, or repair of buildings, in force at the time of loss.
(b) "Undamaged portion" means the part of a covered building structure that did not sustain direct physical loss but must be demolished or altered to comply with an ordinance or law.

CLAUSE 2. INCREASED LIMIT
The Ordinance or Law coverage percentage stated in the base wording is increased from 10 percent to 25 percent of the Coverage A limit of liability shown in the Declarations. This increase applies only to losses occurring on or after the effective date shown above.

CLAUSE 3. COVERED COSTS
The insurer will pay the increased costs the insured actually incurs to: (a) demolish and clear the site of the undamaged portion when required by ordinance or law; (b) rebuild or repair to the minimum standard the ordinance or law requires.

CLAUSE 4. EXCLUSIONS TABLE
The following exclusions apply to this endorsement:

| Code | Exclusion Description | Applies To | Exception |
| E-41 | Costs to comply with any ordinance or law requiring remediation of pollutants, including testing and monitoring | This endorsement | None. Pollutant remediation is excluded absolutely. |
| E-42 | Loss in value of the building due to the requirements of any ordinance or law | This endorsement | None. Diminution in value is not a covered cost. |

CLAUSE 5. OTHER TERMS
All other provisions of the policy remain unchanged.
