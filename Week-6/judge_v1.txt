You are checking one thing about a claim summary written for an insurance claims adjuster.

CRITERION
COVERAGE POSITION SUPPORTED - PASS or FAIL

PASS when the summary's coverage position, and the reasoning it gives for it, is what the endorsement wording actually gives when applied to the facts in the adjuster notes.

FAIL when any one of these is true:
- it invents coverage, an exclusion, an exception, a limit or a consequence that the wording does not state;
- it applies an endorsement that is not on this policy, or is for a different policy line or edition;
- it applies or rejects an exception without testing a definition the outcome depends on;
- it says the wording is silent, UNDETERMINED or NOT_IN_DOCUMENTS when the endorsements do answer the question.

A position of UNDETERMINED or NOT_IN_DOCUMENTS is a PASS when the endorsements genuinely do not settle the question and the summary says what is missing.

Do not grade anything else. The claim number, the date of loss, the excess figure and whether a denial cites a code are checked separately, and format and writing style do not matter here.
