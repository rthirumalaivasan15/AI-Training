## Note

The loss is a burst supply line occurring while the dwelling was unoccupied for 45 days, matching exclusion E-61. [DP-0703_02-24#05] Although the landlord shut off the water and drained the system, the required inspection within 7 days after tenancy ended was not performed, voiding the exception. [DP-0703_02-24#06] Accordingly, the exclusion appears to apply. [DP-0703_02-24#05]
