## Note

The insured reports two feet of water in the finished basement after a creek overtopped its banks, with water entering through the basement window wells. [HO-0304_03-24#02] The policy defines “surface water” as water on the ground surface, including flood and overflow of any body of water, which matches the described loss. [HO-0304_03-24#02] Clause 5 of HO‑0304 lists exclusion E‑15 for flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of any body of water, applying to all Section I coverages with no exception. [HO-0304_03-24#05] Although HO‑0820 provides water‑backup coverage with a $1,000 per‑form deductible and a $10,000 per‑event sub‑limit, it only overrides exclusion E‑16 and does not affect exclusion E‑15.
