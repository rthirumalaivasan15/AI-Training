## Note

The loss involves river flood water entering the ground floor, which is classified as "surface water" under the endorsement (see HO-0304_01-25#02). Exclusion E-15 expressly excludes flood and surface water losses, except for storm ingress defined as water entering during a storm with >50 mm rainfall (HO-0304_01-25#02). No storm rainfall occurred, so the exclusion applies (HO-0304_01-25#05). The Storm Ingress Allowance sub‑limit does not apply (HO-0304_01-25#07).
